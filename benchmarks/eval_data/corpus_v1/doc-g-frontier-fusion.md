# Frontier Research — Room-Temperature Fusion

This document contains publicly confirmed research notes about room-temperature fusion. It is intentionally partial: some topics are covered, others are explicitly NOT covered.

What is known:
- The 2031 "Helion-DARPA cooperative" reported a sustained 45-second run at net energy gain ratio (Q) of 1.4.
- The reactor design uses magnetized target fusion (MTF) rather than tokamak confinement.
- The group published results for 2029 and 2031 runs; no 2030 paper exists.

What is explicitly NOT covered:
- This document does NOT contain any information about cold-fusion lattice experiments (the Holton lattice group).
- This document does NOT state the cost of building the reactor.
- This document does NOT mention the power output in watts.

Topics for which this document has NO data: reactor capital cost, cold-fusion lattice results, watt output, and the 2030 experimental run.
