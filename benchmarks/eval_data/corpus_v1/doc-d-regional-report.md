# Regional Manufacturing Report — 2025

This report summarizes manufacturing activity across the three Acme plant regions in 2025.

## New York Region
The New York plant produced 1.2 million units in 2025. Its flagship line was fasteners. Utilization was 82%.

## Ohio Region
The Ohio plant produced 1.8 million units in 2025. Its flagship line was industrial sealants. Utilization was 91%.

## Mexico Region
The Mexico plant (Monterrey) produced 0.9 million units in 2025. Its flagship line was packaging adhesives. Utilization was 74%.

## Combined
Combined regional output across New York, Ohio, and Mexico in 2025 was 3.9 million units. Ohio had the highest utilization.

The eastern US distribution network is served primarily by the Memphis center, which moved 2.2 million units in 2025.
