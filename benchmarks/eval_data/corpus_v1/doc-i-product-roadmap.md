# Acme Product Roadmap — 2026-2027

This roadmap covers three product lines. It is a planning document and contains forward-looking commitments.

## Adhesives (existing)
- V2 of the industrial sealant, scheduled Q2 2026.
- The V2 sealant improves curing speed by 35% versus V1.

## Fasteners (existing)
- New torque-resistant fastener line, scheduled Q1 2027.
- The new line is targeting a 20% weight reduction.

## Robotics (new)
- Acme enters robotics for the first time with an inspection robot, scheduled Q4 2026.
- The inspection robot is designed for warehouse inventory counting and integrates with the Memphis distribution center systems.

## Key dependencies
- The robotics line depends on the Atlas database engine for its vision-log storage.
- The inspection robot project is gated on the completion of the Delta Sync feature in Atlas.

The 2026-2027 roadmap does not include any plans for consumer electronics.
