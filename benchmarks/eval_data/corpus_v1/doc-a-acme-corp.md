# Acme Corporation — Company Fact File

Acme Corporation was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in New York City.

- Founded: 1987
- Headquarters: New York City
- Number of employees (2025): 12,400
- Annual revenue (2025): $4.7 billion USD
- Primary industry: Industrial adhesives and fasteners
- Publicly traded on: NYSE under ticker "ACME"

The current Chief Executive Officer is Diana Reyes, who took the role in January 2023, succeeding founder Martin Whitfield.

Acme's fastest growing product line in 2025 was the industrial sealant division, which grew 23% year over year.

Acme operates manufacturing plants in New York, Ohio, and Mexico.
