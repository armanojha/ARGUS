# Acme Manufacturing Timeline — Overview

This document provides a high-level overview of Acme manufacturing. Note that detailed, authoritative figures about Acme plants and regions appear in several SEPARATE documents (the Regional Manufacturing Report, the Operational Metrics report, and the 2025 Revenue report). This overview is only a summary.

- Acme runs three primary plants: New York, Ohio, and Mexico (Monterrey).
- Production at the Ohio plant has grown strongly and reached its highest utilization in 2025.
- Combined regional manufacturing output is tracked in the 2025 Regional Manufacturing Report.
- Ohio is upstream of the Mexico plant in the supply network and feeds the Memphis distribution center.

For precise 2025 output numbers, utilization figures, and revenue, you must consult the dedicated reports: the Regional Manufacturing Report, the Q3 Operational Metrics report, and the 2025 Revenue report.
