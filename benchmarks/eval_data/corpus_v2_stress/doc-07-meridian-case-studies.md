# Meridian Customer Case Studies

## Case Study 1: GlobalAuto Manufacturing

### Background
GlobalAuto, a Fortune 500 automotive manufacturer, implemented Meridian's Manufacturing IoT platform to modernize their 12 plants across 8 countries.

### Challenge
- Legacy manufacturing systems with 15+ year old equipment
- No real-time visibility into production quality
- 3.2% defect rate (industry average: 1.5%)
- $45M annual waste from quality issues

### Solution
- Deployed SensorNet across 12 plants (2.4M sensors)
- Implemented AutomaBot-200 for quality inspection
- Integrated with Meridian Cloud Platform for analytics
- Custom AI models for predictive maintenance

### Results (18 months post-deployment)
- Defect rate: 3.2% → 0.8% (75% reduction)
- Waste reduction: $45M → $12M annually
- OEE improvement: 78% → 91%
- Unplanned downtime: 4.2% → 1.1%
- ROI: 340% over 3 years

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Defect rate | 3.2% | 0.8% | -75% |
| OEE | 78% | 91% | +16.7% |
| Downtime | 4.2% | 1.1% | -73.8% |
| Annual waste | $45M | $12M | -73.3% |
| Energy usage | 100% | 82% | -18% |

### Lessons Learned
1. **Phased rollout**: Start with highest-impact plants
2. **Legacy integration**: Custom adapters needed for 40% of equipment
3. **Change management**: Operator training critical for success
4. **Data quality**: 6-month data cleaning before AI models effective

## Case Study 2: HealthFirst Hospital Network

### Background
HealthFirst operates 24 hospitals and 180 clinics across the southeastern United States, serving 2.8 million patients annually.

### Challenge
- Fragmented IT systems across 24 hospitals
- Patient data silos preventing coordinated care
- Compliance burden (HIPAA, HITECH)
- Rising costs with flat reimbursement rates

### Solution
- Meridian Cloud Platform (FedRAMP High)
- SecureVault IAM for patient data access
- DataFlow Analytics for population health
- Custom integration with Epic EHR system

### Results (24 months post-deployment)
- Care coordination: 34% improvement in chronic disease management
- Compliance: 100% audit pass rate
- Cost reduction: $18M annually from operational efficiency
- Patient satisfaction: 78% → 89%

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Care coordination | Baseline | +34% | Significant |
| Compliance audits | 94% pass | 100% pass | +6pp |
| Operating costs | $420M | $402M | -4.3% |
| Patient satisfaction | 78% | 89% | +11pp |
| Readmission rate | 12.4% | 9.8% | -21% |

### Lessons Learned
1. **Integration complexity**: 18 months longer than planned
2. **Data migration**: Required dedicated team of 15 for 6 months
3. **Regulatory approval**: FedRAMP authorization took 9 months
4. **Clinical adoption**: Required physician champions

## Case Study 3: GlobalBank Financial Services

### Background
GlobalBank, a top-20 global bank, deployed Meridian's security platform after a series of sophisticated cyberattacks.

### Challenge
- 2.3 billion daily transactions to monitor
- State-sponsored threat actors
- 45-minute mean time to detect (MTTD)
- Regulatory pressure (SOX, PCI DSS, GDPR)

### Solution
- Project Sentinel AI security platform
- Meridian Cloud for threat intelligence
- SecureVault for privileged access
- Custom threat hunting dashboards

### Results (12 months post-deployment)
- MTTD: 45 minutes → 2.3 minutes (95% reduction)
- False positives: 12% → 0.3% (97.5% reduction)
- Threat detection: 847 → 2,341 (176% increase)
- Compliance: Zero regulatory findings

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| MTTD | 45 min | 2.3 min | -95% |
| False positives | 12% | 0.3% | -97.5% |
| Threats detected | 847/year | 2,341/year | +176% |
| Analyst productivity | 15 alerts/day | 150 alerts/day | +900% |
| Response time | 4.2 hours | 18 minutes | -93% |

### Lessons Learned
1. **Talent shortage**: Required external security team for first 6 months
2. **Integration**: 300+ legacy systems required custom connectors
3. **Tuning**: 3-month tuning period to reduce false positives
4. **Board support**: Required executive sponsorship for $12M investment

## Case Study 4: NordicClean Energy

### Background
NordicClean, a Scandinavian renewable energy company, uses Meridian's edge computing platform for wind farm optimization.

### Challenge
- 1,200 wind turbines across 8 wind farms
- Remote locations with limited connectivity
- Variable wind conditions requiring real-time optimization
- $200M annual revenue at risk from downtime

### Solution
- Meridian Edge Compute Engine (ECE) at each turbine
- SensorNet for vibration and performance monitoring
- AI models for predictive maintenance
- Cloud integration for fleet-wide optimization

### Results (12 months post-deployment)
- Energy output: +12% from optimized turbine control
- Downtime: 8.4% → 2.1% (75% reduction)
- Maintenance costs: $45M → $32M annually
- Predictive accuracy: 94% for component failures

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Energy output | 100% | 112% | +12% |
| Downtime | 8.4% | 2.1% | -75% |
| Maintenance costs | $45M | $32M | -28.9% |
| Predictive accuracy | N/A | 94% | New capability |
| Grid stability | 97.2% | 99.1% | +1.9pp |

### Lessons Learned
1. **Connectivity**: Satellite backup essential for remote locations
2. **Power constraints**: Edge devices required custom low-power design
3. **Environmental**: -40°C to +60°C operating range required
4. **ROI timeline**: 18-month payback period
