### Supply Chain Risks (Q3 2025)

#### Critical Risks
1. **Semiconductor shortage**: Lead times extended to 26 weeks for custom chips
2. **Geopolitical tensions**: Taiwan strait shipping routes at risk
3. **Raw material costs**: Rare earth prices up 34% YoY
4. **Climate events**: Hurricane season threatening Gulf Coast logistics

#### Mitigation Strategies
1. **Dual sourcing**: All critical components have 2+ suppliers
2. **Strategic inventory**: 90-day buffer for high-risk components
3. **Regional diversification**: Manufacturing spread across 3 continents
4. **Long-term contracts**: Lock in pricing for 18-24 months
