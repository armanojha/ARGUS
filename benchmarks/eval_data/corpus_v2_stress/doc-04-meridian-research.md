# Meridian Research & Frontier Technologies

## Project Portfolio

### Active Projects (Q3 2025)

#### 1. Project Catalyst (Quantum Computing)
- **Status**: Active development
- **Goal**: 10,000-qubit processor by 2027
- **Current milestone**: 1,200 qubits achieved (Q2 2025)
- **Team size**: 340 researchers
- **Budget**: $1.8 billion (2024-2027)
- **Key breakthrough**: Error correction reduced from 1,000:1 to 100:1 physical-to-logical qubit ratio

#### 2. Project Neural Link (Brain-Computer Interface)
- **Status**: Preclinical
- **Goal**: Non-invasive neural interface for AR/VR
- **Current milestone**: 96-channel EEG headset prototype
- **Team size**: 180 researchers
- **Budget**: $890 million (2024-2028)
- **Timeline**: Human trials expected Q4 2026

#### 3. Project Titan (Advanced Materials)
- **Status**: Material discovery phase
- **Goal**: Room-temperature superconductor
- **Current milestone**: Candidate materials identified (3 families)
- **Team size**: 120 researchers
- **Budget**: $450 million (2024-2029)
- **Note**: No viable candidates yet; speculative research

#### 4. Project Sentinel (AI Security)
- **Status**: Production deployment
- **Goal**: AI-powered threat detection and response
- **Current milestone**: Processing 2.3 billion events/day
- **Team size**: 280 researchers
- **Budget**: $620 million (2024-2026)
- **Performance**: 99.7% detection rate, 0.3% false positives

#### 5. Project Horizon (Edge AI)
- **Status**: Beta testing
- **Goal**: On-device AI processing without cloud dependency
- **Current milestone**: 15 TOPS/W efficiency achieved
- **Team size**: 160 researchers
- **Budget**: $340 million (2024-2026)
- **Partners**: NVIDIA (GPU architecture), Qualcomm (mobile chips)

## Challenges & Risks

### Technical Challenges
1. **Quantum decoherence**: Current qubits maintain coherence for 100 microseconds (need 10ms)
2. **AI model size**: GPT-5 equivalent requires 500GB memory (need <100GB)
3. **Material synthesis**: Room-temperature superconductor candidates require extreme conditions

### Resource Constraints
1. **Talent shortage**: 200 unfilled research positions
2. **Equipment costs**: Quantum dilution refrigerators cost $5M each
3. **Computing resources**: 50,000 GPU-hours/day for AI training

### Competitive Landscape
- **IBM**: 4,334-qubit processor (Condor)
- **Google**: Quantum supremacy demonstrations
- **Microsoft**: Topological qubits research
- **Intel**: Silicon spin qubits

## Controversies

### Ethics Concerns (Neural Link)
In June 2025, the Electronic Frontier Foundation raised concerns about Project Neural Link's potential for surveillance and manipulation. Meridian established an external ethics board in response.

### Environmental Impact (Quantum Computing)
Project Catalyst's dilution refrigerators consume 1.2 MW each. Environmental groups criticize the energy-intensive research, though Meridian argues the long-term efficiency gains will offset current consumption.

### Patent Thicket
Meridian's aggressive patent filing has been criticized as creating a "patent thicket" that stifles competition in quantum computing and AI security.
